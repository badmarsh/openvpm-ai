# Reports

The Reports section (`/reports`) gives you financial, operational, and
statutory compliance insights across your practice. Reports are available
to **Admin** and **Veterinarian** roles only.

> **Cloud note**: Advanced Reporting is a Cloud feature on hosted OpenVPM.
> Self-hosted instances have unrestricted access to all reports.

---

## 1. Overview

Open **Reports** (`/reports`) from the sidebar. Each report has a
**date range selector** at the top — choose from preset ranges (today,
this week, this month, last month, last 90 days) or enter a custom start
and end date.

Reports compare the selected period to the equivalent previous period so
you can see trends at a glance.

---

## 2. Revenue

The **Revenue** report shows:

- Total revenue from paid invoices in the selected period
- Comparison to the previous equivalent period (absolute and percentage)
- Day-by-day revenue chart for the selected range (zero-filled — every day
  appears on the chart even with no revenue, so gaps are visible)

Use the date range to identify seasonal trends or compare quarters.

---

## 3. Appointments

The **Appointments** report shows:

- Total appointments in the period
- Breakdown by status: completed, no-show, cancelled, rescheduled
- No-show rate percentage
- Provider utilisation (appointments per provider)

---

## 4. Service performance

The **Services** report breaks down revenue and volume by service and
product. Use it to identify your highest-margin services, most-used
products, and underperforming areas.

---

## 5. Inventory

The **Inventory** report shows stock levels, usage rates, and expiry
alerts across your product catalogue. Filter by category or alert type
(low stock, expiring soon, expired).

---

## 6. Statutory compliance

The **Statutory** report provides summaries of your statutory registers
for the selected period:

- Rabies vaccinations recorded and RVPS notifications sent
- Withdrawal period records for food-producing animals
- Controlled substance dispensings (Admin and Veterinarian only)

Use this report when preparing for ŠVPS SR inspections or internal audits.

---

## 7. Export

Click **Export** on any report to download a CSV file of the underlying
data. Use the CSV for further analysis in Excel or your accounting software.

> ⚠️ **PDF export — Slovak diacritics**: PDF report exports run through a
> sanitiser that strips Slovak diacritics (č→c, š→s, ä→a, ľ→l). Use CSV
> export when you need exact character preservation for official documents.

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

**Report shows no data:**
- Check that the date range includes a period with actual activity
- Confirm your practice timezone is set correctly in **Settings → Practice
  info** — reports group revenue and appointments by local date/time
