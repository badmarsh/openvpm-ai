# Scope Clarifications

**Commit:** `65e008d` | **Date:** 2026-09-14

Authoritative scope statements for every module where naming, boundary, or purpose was found to be ambiguous. The H6 boundary (Záznamy vs Vyšetrenia vs Zdravotné pripomienky vs treatment plans) is addressed first and in full, as it motivated this entire audit.

---

## §1 — H6: Definitive Boundary Between Clinical Record Modules

This is the seed question. The answer is: **all four concepts are architecturally distinct; the confusion is naming and placement, not underlying implementation**.

### Záznamy → "Klinická karta" (Patient Medical Record)

**What it is:**  
The complete, patient-level historical view of all clinical entries for a selected patient. Read-optimized. Used by vets and front desk to review a patient's full history across all past visits. Contains: all SOAP notes (finalized, read-only), all vaccinations, all prescriptions, all lab results, all problems, all procedures.

**What it is NOT:**  
It is not where you write clinical entries during an active visit. Writing happens in Vyšetrenia (the encounter workspace). Záznamy is where you go to read history, not to create a new record.

**Router backing:** `trpc.records.*` (4,878-line monolith — reading from `soap_notes`, `vaccinations`, `prescriptions`, `lab_results`, `problems`, `procedures`) [VERIFIED: records/page.tsx:795–943]

**Proposed scope statement:**  
> Klinická karta = the complete read-only historical record of every clinical event for a specific patient, spanning all past visits. If you are writing a new clinical entry during today's visit, you are in Vyšetrenia.

---

### Vyšetrenia → "Encounter Workspace" (Active Visit Management)

**What it is:**  
The visit-level, write-enabled workspace. A vet opens a specific appointment, records vitals, writes the SOAP note for that encounter, manages the treatment plan for this visit, orders labs, records procedures, and closes out the visit. The encounter is time-bounded to a single appointment.

**What it is NOT:**  
It is not a historical browser. You don't browse all past encounters here — you manage the current one. You cannot edit a finalized SOAP note here (correction goes through a correction procedure).

**Router backing:** `trpc.encounters.*` + `trpc.appointments.*` (creating the visit context) [VERIFIED: encounters/page.tsx:51–64]

**Proposed scope statement:**  
> Vyšetrenia = the active or recently active visit workspace. Each encounter is one visit/appointment. This is where SOAP notes are written, vitals recorded, procedures ordered, and visits closed out. Historical SOAP notes are in Klinická karta.

---

### Zdravotné pripomienky → "Preventívna starostlivosť" (Preventive Care Task List)

**What it is:**  
A patient-level proactive care task list. Each entry is a specific care need for a specific patient (e.g., "Fluffy — rabies vaccine overdue"). Front desk or vets can send outreach (SMS/email) to the client, mark as complete, or dismiss. These are generated by the care reminder engine and by clinical entries (vaccination records trigger due-date reminders).

**What it is NOT:**  
It is not a scheduling tool (you don't book appointments here). It is not a batch messaging tool (you send individual outreach, not bulk messages). It is not a medical record (care reminders are workflow items, not clinical entries).

**Router backing:** `trpc.careReminders.*` on `care_reminders` table [VERIFIED: care-reminders/page.tsx:86]

**Proposed scope statement:**  
> Preventívna starostlivosť = a per-patient care task list showing which patients need proactive attention (overdue vaccines, upcoming procedures). Each task is acted on individually — send reminder, book appointment, or dismiss. Batch messaging is in Hromadný recall (tab).

---

### Pripomienky → "Hromadný recall" (Batch Vaccination Recall)

**What it is:**  
A cross-patient batch operation for vaccination recall messaging. The page generates a preview list of all patients overdue for specific vaccines, then sends batch SMS/email reminders to their owners in one operation.

**What it is NOT:**  
It is not per-patient (you don't manage individual patients here). It is not a clinical record. It is not the same as sending a care reminder to one patient.

**Router backing:** `trpc.notifications.getVaccinationRecallPreview` + `.sendVaccinationReminders` [VERIFIED: recalls/page.tsx:51–74]

**Proposed scope statement:**  
> Hromadný recall = a batch cross-patient vaccination recall tool. Use it once a week/month to identify all patients overdue for core vaccines and send mass reminders in one click. For individual patient reminders, use Preventívna starostlivosť.

---

### Treatment Plans (trpc.treatmentPlans.*) — NOT a nav item

**What it is:**  
Longitudinal clinical care plans attached to a patient's problem list. They span multiple visits and describe ongoing clinical management of a chronic condition or post-surgical recovery. Accessed within the encounter workspace or patient record — not a top-level nav destination.

**What it is NOT:**  
It is NOT the same as Treatment Estimates (formerly: visit-treatment-plans). The naming confusion was documented in REORGANIZATION-FINDINGS.md §10 [VERIFIED] and already resolved in `_app.ts` (renamed to `treatmentEstimatesRouter`).

**Proposed scope statement:**  
> Treatment Plans (Plány starostlivosti) = longitudinal, multi-visit clinical care plans for ongoing conditions. They live inside the patient's record and encounter workspace — not a standalone nav item. Treatment Estimates (Cenové ponuky) = client-facing, single-visit cost proposals with client e-signature. Architecturally unrelated despite the similar name.

## §2 — Other Module Scope Clarifications

### Laboratórium vs lab_results in Záznamy

**Záznamy** shows the per-patient lab result HISTORY (all past results for one patient, read-only, trend charts).

**Laboratórium** is the clinic-wide lab result REVIEW INBOX (pending results that need a vet to review and complete, cross-patient, workflow-oriented).

> Lab výsledky (nav item) = technician/vet review queue for pending lab results. Per-patient lab history is in Klinická karta.

---

### Správy (inbox `/inbox`) vs Správy & SMS (`/marketing/messages`)

**Správy (inbox):** Two-way conversational messaging with individual clients. Threads, read/unread status, portal integration. Backed by `conversations` + `messages` tables.

**Kampane & SMS (was: Správy & SMS):** Outbound marketing SMS campaigns and broadcast messages. No threading. Backed by `ext_marketing_sms_log`.

These are completely different products sharing a confusingly similar Slovak name. The marketing item must be renamed.

> Správy (inbox) = conversational client messaging, one thread per client. Kampane & SMS = outbound marketing broadcast messages to client segments.

---

### Čakáreň vs Prevádzková tabuľa

**Čakáreň (`/waiting-room`):** Patient-facing display. Shows checked-in patients to people sitting in the waiting area. Minimal information, TV-optimized, read-only.

**Prevádzková tabuľa (`/whiteboard`):** Staff operational tool. Shows ALL active visits with status transitions, doctor assignments, visit duration. Click-to-update status. Staff-facing.

Both use `trpc.whiteboard.*` but serve completely different audiences and interaction patterns. Not the same feature.

> Čakáreň = passive waiting room display for clients. Prevádzková tabuľa = active staff operations board for managing patient flow.

---

### Zákonné registre vs Omamné látky

**Zákonné registre (`/statutory`):** Statutory compliance registers mandated by ŠVPS SR and KVL SR: Rabies (Kniha besnoty), Treatment Diary (Kniha ošetrení), Euthanasia register, CRSZ chip lookup, KVEPIS B2G.

**Omamné látky (`/controlled-substances`):** Controlled substance dispensing ledger with witness enforcement. Legally required under Zákon 139/1998 Z.z.

Omamné látky IS technically a statutory register, but its workflow (dispensing/wasting with witness co-signing) is distinctly pharmacy-operational. Keeping it in the Pharmacy section is operationally correct; a cross-reference link to the statutory page is needed for compliance context.

> Zákonné registre = read-and-report compliance registers (rabies, euthanasia, treatment diary). Omamné látky = active dispensing ledger with witness enforcement — a pharmacy workflow that also fulfills a statutory obligation.

---

### Vzdialená Podpora vs Admin Podpora (H3 scope clarification)

**Vzdialená Podpora (`/support`):** The clinic's "call for help" interface. Any staff member creates a session and receives a code to share with VET.IS support.

**Admin Podpora (`/admin/support`):** The VET.IS INTERNAL support engineer interface to join a clinic's session. NOT a clinic admin tool despite the "Admin" prefix.

The "Admin" in "Admin Podpora" refers to VET.IS platform admin, not the clinic's Practice Admin role — this naming is the source of confusion.

> Vzdialená Podpora = clinic-facing "request support" tool. Admin Podpora = VET.IS-internal support engineer join interface. Should not appear in clinic nav.

---

### Fakturácia vs e-Kasa Doklady

**Fakturácia:** Invoice and payment management — create invoices, record payments, manage overdue. Slovak PIMS billing ledger.

**e-Kasa Doklady:** Slovak fiscal receipt issuance to FR SR (Finančná správa). An e-Kasa receipt is triggered FROM a paid invoice but is a separate legal document sent to the tax authority.

> Fakturácia = internal billing ledger. e-Kasa = government fiscal receipts (separate legal obligation under Zákon 289/2008 Z.z.).
